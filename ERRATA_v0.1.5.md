# Consolidated Errata for v0.1.5

Version 0.1.5 remains preserved as the historical initial public release.

The following methods are superseded and must not be used for decisions:

1. The published readiness weights totaled 110 percent.
2. The aggregate risk method averaged unlike factors and could conceal critical command, safety, recovery, or evidence conditions.
3. The narrative example did not provide a reproducible calculation trail.
4. The former highest maturity label could be read as an absolute quantum-safety claim.

Version 0.2.0 replaces those methods with separately oriented exposure, readiness, evidence confidence and coverage, and non-compensatory critical conditions. It does not produce an automated authorization or certification result.
