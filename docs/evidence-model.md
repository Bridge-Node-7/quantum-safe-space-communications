# Evidence Model

The framework distinguishes five questions:

1. What claim is being made?
2. Which scope item does it affect?
3. What evidence supports it?
4. How strong, current, independent, and applicable is that evidence?
5. What remains unknown, stale, conflicting, withdrawn, or excluded?

Confidence level, provenance, independence, currency, and coverage are separate fields. An E4 record may still be stale or too narrow for the claim. A current E2 record may be useful but insufficient for a deployment claim.

Critical-path contradictions must be escalated under CR-12 until reconciled and recorded.
