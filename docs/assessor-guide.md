# Assessor Guide

## Before starting

Confirm authorization, scope owner, information-handling rules, assessor competence, review roles, and the intended decision. Use fictional data in public examples.

## Assessment discipline

- Trace each material statement to evidence.
- Keep Unknown separate from Not Applicable.
- Record evidence provenance, independence, currency, scope, and conflicts.
- Escalate any possible critical condition immediately.
- Do not convert a planning posture into authorization language.
- Record assumptions, exclusions, dissent, and limitations.

## Review quality

A second reviewer should challenge scope completeness, critical-path coverage, evidence sufficiency, ownership, and the consistency of the Quantum Readiness Decision Pack. Independent validation is claimed only when the reviewer, qualifications, scope, evidence, limitations, conflicts, and decision are recorded.
