# Quantum Readiness Decision Pack

## Purpose

The Quantum Readiness Decision Pack is the complete human-reviewed deliverable. It assembles scope, technical evidence, critical conditions, ownership, actions, decisions, approvals, limitations, and review history.

## Required artifacts

1. Scope and system/link map
2. Cryptographic inventory
3. Quantum Exposure Severity records
4. Ten-domain Migration Readiness Profile
5. Evidence Confidence and Coverage summary and ledger
6. Critical Risk Override register
7. Ownership and action plan
8. Vendor and standards applicability summary
9. Migration and recovery roadmap
10. Executive decision record, approvals, limitations, and review history

## Assembly order

Build the artifacts in the listed order. Cross-reference stable IDs for scope items, evidence, exposure, overrides, vendors, and actions.

## Minimum quality gates

- Unknowns and exclusions are explicit.
- Not Applicable entries have evidence and accountable approval.
- Evidence coverage and critical unknowns are reported.
- All twelve critical conditions are considered.
- Open critical conditions have owners and dates.
- No weighted readiness total or compensating average is used.
- Decision-support language remains non-authoritative.
- Limitations and reassessment triggers are recorded.

## Approval and review

Use [Decision Summary](decision-summary-template.md) and [Review Record](review-record-template.md). Preserve dissent and accepted limitations.

## Retention

Store real Decision Packs only in an approved controlled environment. The public repository contains fictional examples and blank templates only.
