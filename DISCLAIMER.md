# Disclaimer

Quantum Readiness for Space Communications is a defensive assessment and migration-planning framework.

It does not:

- certify that a system is quantum-safe;
- determine legal or regulatory compliance;
- provide operational authorization or authorization to deploy;
- provide flight qualification;
- replace cryptographic engineering, mission assurance, safety review, or legal advice;
- validate a vendor product or implementation;
- predict when a cryptographically relevant quantum computer will exist.

Outputs depend on the scope, evidence, assumptions, and assessor judgment supplied. Unknown or unavailable information must remain visible and must not be treated as favorable evidence.

Use the framework only with authorized information. Protect mission, vendor, customer, and security-sensitive data.

This framework does not provide certification or operational authorization.
