# Quantum Readiness for Space Communications v0.2.0

Documentation-first, publicly distributable framework for assembling a **Quantum Readiness Decision Pack** across spacecraft, links, ground systems, mission operations, and vendors.

## Primary corrections

- Retires the v0.1.5 weighted aggregate and compensating average risk method
- Adds Unknown / Not Assessed exposure handling
- Separates exposure, ten-domain readiness, Evidence Confidence and Coverage, and twelve critical conditions
- Preserves v0.1.5 history and publishes consolidated errata
- Excludes the withdrawn assessment engine and workbook calculator

This release is not certification, operational authorization, compliance determination, flight qualification, or proof that a system is quantum-safe.
